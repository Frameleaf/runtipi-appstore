## Your photos. Your server. Everywhere.

Frameleaf is a photo and video library that runs on your own server. Your originals stay as ordinary files on your own disks. Faces, smart search and captions are worked out on your own hardware, and there is no telemetry.

- Native apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Apple TV and Android TV, and a desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux
- Automatic phone backup, albums, sharing with partners and public links
- Face recognition, smart search, places and duplicates
- A photo editor, Studio for video, Boards for references, and client galleries for photographers

Frameleaf is in beta: keep another copy of your photos and videos.

On first launch, create the admin account in the browser. There are no default credentials. Photos are stored in `media/data/images/frameleaf` in your Runtipi folder. Frameleaf needs about 6 GB of RAM and 2 CPU cores; the machine-learning service downloads its models the first time faces and smart search run.
